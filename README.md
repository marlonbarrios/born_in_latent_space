# Born in Latent Space

*Born in Latent Space* is a lecture-performance, installation, and workshop platform—an ongoing artistic and research project (2024–2028) that I develop as a living, evolving system rather than a fixed work. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}  

At its core, it is a performance-lecture in which I appear with a laptop, voice, body, and a constellation of custom-built and generative AI applications, engaging in real time with machine processes. However, this format is only one possible manifestation. The work can expand into an installation, unfold as a participatory workshop, or operate as a hybrid environment where these modalities overlap and inform one another.

The project is composed as a network of fragments—vignettes, prompts, scores, applications, texts, and generative systems—that I continuously rearrange and recombine. Each iteration is a different configuration, a different trajectory through latent space. Rather than following a linear structure, the work operates as a recursive and modular architecture in which elements circulate, reappear, and transform across contexts.

Within this shifting system, I perform through a fully embodied practice that integrates storytelling, singing, movement, and at times DJing or VJing, alongside the live use of real-time computational and AI-based tools. Voice, gesture, and physical action function as interfaces, as modes of thinking and sensing within a dynamic exchange between human and machine. The body does not represent the system; it participates in it, entangled in processes of generation, translation, and response.

The work weaves together layers of fiction and lived experience, drawing from histories of migration, cybernetics, and complexity. These narrative and conceptual strands are not presented as stable content but as materials in flux, recomposed through performance. They unfold in relation to multiple forms of symbiosis—between human and machine, memory and speculation, embodiment and abstraction, individual and collective cognition.

As an installation, *Born in Latent Space* becomes spatial and distributed, with autonomous processes, screens, and interfaces that extend beyond the temporal frame of performance. As a workshop, it opens into a shared laboratory where participants engage directly with its tools, logics, and questions, activating their own relationships to AI, embodiment, and knowledge production.

Across all its forms, the project functions as a performative infrastructure: a space where ideas are enacted, tested, and transformed through action. It is not a fixed piece but a terrain—an evolving system that I enter, navigate, and reconfigure each time, in relation to machines, environments, and others.
